Copy this file to assignment.txt and edit it each day (or leave assignment.txt
absent for general feedback). The kiosk reads it at startup and uses it to
focus the AI's feedback on today's assignment. A few plain sentences work best.

Example for a lesson like Unit 1, Lesson 01 (the 1763 crisis):

Today's assignment is the Lesson 01 Atlas Worksheets, sections 1-6 on the
French & Indian War, plus the MCAP historical reasoning check. Students were
assigned sections 1-6 only; other pages are not assigned. For the MCAP check,
look for: a clear claim about how the war produced the imperial crisis, at
least two supporting details that name their sources (map B, chart D, or
graph F), the cause-and-effect mechanism, and one qualification or limit of
the evidence. Common issue to watch for: students citing evidence without
naming which map or graph it came from.
